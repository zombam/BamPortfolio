I'm Bam — a London-based Creative Technologist and designer. My graphic and motion work is built with the same rigour as my code: systematic, research-grounded, and biased toward the unexpected.

Six years across three studios — DesignFinn, BrightDolphins, and OFF.live — covering brand identity, UX/UI, motion graphics, and 3D. B.A. Fine & Applied Arts, Chulalongkorn University (2nd Class Honours, 2019).
