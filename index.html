I'm Bam — a London-based Creative Technologist whose practice sits at the intersection of real-time computation, embodied interaction, and visual craft.

My research into octopus neurology and decentralised nervous systems shapes both the H.A.N.D. thesis and the Multi-Minded Shape — systems that think with their periphery, not their centre.

M.Sc. Creative Computing, UAL (Distinction, 2025). B.A. Fine & Applied Arts, Chulalongkorn University (2nd Class Honours, 2019).
