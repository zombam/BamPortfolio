The technologist

Code that thinks
with its periphery

My creative computing practice centres on a single question: what happens when intelligence is distributed rather than centralised? The H.A.N.D., Marine Pulse, and Coral Bleaching all explore this — each builds systems that negotiate meaning locally, without a commanding brain.

I build with TouchDesigner, Python APIs, physical sensors, and generative AI — always grounded in a biological or ecological concept that makes the technical choices feel necessary rather than arbitrary.
